# Roadmap

Estimates are rough guesses and depend on maintainer availability. Phases are strictly sequential: nothing in a later phase starts until the previous phase is released and has been tried by someone other than the maintainer.

| Phase | Milestone        | Scope                                                | Rough estimate |
| ----- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| 0     | v0.0 Foundation  | Tooling, CI, attestation registry, indexer           | 1-2 weeks      |
| 1     | v0.1 Credentials | Issue, revoke, verify; SDK; issuer and verifier apps | 3-4 weeks      |
| 2     | v0.2 Reputation  | GitHub App, scoring, profiles                        | about 3 weeks  |
| 3     | v0.3 Funding     | Rounds, quadratic matching, Sybil resistance         | about 4 weeks  |
| 4     | v0.4 Governance  | Voting, timelock, optional off-chain voting          | about 4 weeks  |

A phase is done when its contracts are tested and deployed to testnet, documented, its SDK is released, and at least one other person has tried it and reported feedback.
